# Prices and credit

## Selling prices

- Rate butter and chocolate revised the 1er of each month (butter rate, cocoa price), sent to customers the 25 of the previous month.
- Flours: quarterly price indexed on milled wheat; possible annual price agreements for large bakers.
- Frozen, Packed and Grocery: Annual rate at 1 February.
- Franco 150 € excl. VAT, otherwise 12 € shipping costs.
- Discounts: commercial up to 5 %, beyond Bernard. The standard cannot grant a discount.

Order of resolution: negotiated customer price > price rule (customer, then group) > price list.

## Customer groups

- **Boulangeries** (`boulangeries`): Artisan bakeries: sack flour, yeast, laminating butter, packaging.
- **Passeries** (`patisseries`): Pastries and chocolate makers: blankets, cream, dried fruits; peaks at Easter and Christmas.
- **Restaurants** (`chr`): Restaurants and bistros: cream, eggs, butter, some frozen.
- **Snacking** (`snacking`): Franchises and sandwiches: raw cardboard pastries.
- **GMS** (`gms`): Bread strips and cooking terminals from supermarkets.
- **Treaters** (`traiteurs`): Event processors, irregular volumes.
- **Hotel ** (`hotellerie`): Hotels: breakfast, monthly invoices.
- **Collectivities** (`collectivites`): Central kitchens and public establishments.
- **Public market** (`marche-public`): Price per voucher, payment by administrative order.
- **Large accounts** (`grands-comptes`): The largest turnover of the 12 last month, followed by management.
- **To be monitored** (`a-surveiller`): Payment delays, disputes or blocked account: check the outstanding amount before any order.
- **Lost customers** (`perdus`): More orders for several months (competition parties or closed): to win back.

## Credit and collections

- Boulangers in LCR to 30 days end of month; new customers in cash for three months.
- Relaunch to J+5 by email, J+15 by telephone (Sandrine), J+30 by e-mail.
- Automatic locking at the second LCR rejected or above the current ceiling.
- Unblocking: settlement of the due date or schedule signed by Bernard; cash deliveries pending.
