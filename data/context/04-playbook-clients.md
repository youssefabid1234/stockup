# Playbook customer relationship

## Our customers

- **Artisan bakeries** (38 customers) : Traditional flour of Lauragais only. AOP laminating butter for pastry, pastry butter for the rest.
- **Pâtisseries, chocolatiers et salons de thé** (12 customers): The same cover, same origin. Red fruits from Lot-et-Garonne in season.
- **Restaurants and bistros** (15 customers): Cream and eggs twice a week. pains au chocolat raw for Sunday brunch.
- **Sandwicheries and snacking franchises** (8 customers): Order on the portal. Wants a fixed cardboard price over the year.
- **Bread rolls and supermarket cooking terminals** (10 customers): Order by mail with the store order number. Delivery to the dock before 8h.
- **Reception operators and organizers** (8 customers): Large orders to J-2 for events. Mini-pastries for seminars.
- **Hotels (breakfast and restaurant)** (7 guests): Mini-pastries and half-salt butter serving for the buffet. Monthly invoices with internal voucher number.
- **Central and community kitchens** (6 customers): Liquid products only (HACCP). Order form to J-3.

## Taking an order

- Identify the client (name, code, or name of the person calling).
- Propose "as usual" from the last orders, then have the quantities specified.
- Check the stock available and the day's breaks.
- Read the order (products, quantities, day of delivery) before confirming.
- Check the stock and status of the account (locked?) before validating.

## Substitutions: never without agreement

A missing product is only replaced if the customer accepts it. Some customers refuse any substitution (see their sheet): call them AVANT the departure of the truck.

## Blocked customers

No delivery until accounting (Sandrine (Accounting)) has lifted the blockage. Take the message, promise nothing.

## Complaints and returns

- Any complaint is recorded (customer event) with the relevant order.
- A refusal to deliver is taken over by the driver and deducted from the invoice.
- A asset beyond 150 € excl. VAT must be validated by management.
